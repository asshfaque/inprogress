Corporate Tax

UAE Corporate Tax

UAE corporate tax is a federal tax on business profits, administered by the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). Whether and how it applies to you depends on your legal form, activities, income and the rules in force for your tax period.

Who Needs to Register?

  • Companies and other legal entities
  • Certain individuals carrying on a business activity
  • Free zone entities, even where a special regime may apply

Registration

Registration is completed with the FTA online. We help gather company, licence and ownership documents and prepare your details.

Filing

Returns are filed for each tax period, usually supported by financial statements. Deadlines are set by the authority.

Compliance

Keeping records, tracking deadlines and responding to authority requests on time.

Small Business Considerations

Relief may be available to eligible small businesses, subject to conditions and time limits. Eligibility must be checked against current rules.

Free Zone Considerations

Free zone companies may fall within scope. A special regime may apply to qualifying free zone persons on qualifying income, subject to conditions.

Rates, thresholds, reliefs and deadlines can change. Check the current rules with the Federal Tax Authority or a qualified tax adviser before relying on this page.
