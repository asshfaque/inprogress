Offshore Setup

Offshore Company Formation

Understand what an offshore company is, who it may suit and what it cannot do, before you decide.

What Is an Offshore Company?

An offshore company is registered in the UAE to hold assets, shares or investments, or to do business outside the UAE. It is generally not licensed to trade directly in the UAE market. Rules depend on the registering authority.

Who Should Consider It?

  • Investors holding shares, property or other assets
  • Owners structuring international holdings
  • Businesses operating mainly outside the UAE

Benefits

  • Flexible structure for holding and international activity
  • Possible ownership confidentiality, subject to law and compliance rules
  • Simpler setup than some operating structures

Limitations

  • Typically restricted from trading inside the UAE
  • An offshore company alone does not usually provide UAE residency
  • Ongoing compliance, substance and reporting rules may apply

Tax treatment depends on your residency, activities, ownership and the laws that apply to you. An offshore structure is not automatically tax-free, and we do not promise tax outcomes. Take professional tax advice.

Required Documents (indicative)

  • Passport copies of shareholders and directors
  • Proof of residential address
  • Proposed company names
  • Details of the intended activity and ownership structure
  • Additional due-diligence documents requested by the authority or agent

Offshore FAQs

Can an offshore company trade in the UAE?

Generally not. Offshore companies are typically restricted from trading directly in the local market. A mainland or free zone licence may suit local activity better.

Can an offshore company get a UAE residence visa?

An offshore company alone does not usually provide UAE residence. Visa options depend on other factors, such as an operating licence, property or other routes, and on the authority.

Is an offshore company tax-free?

Not automatically. Tax treatment depends on where the owners are resident, what the company does and the laws that apply. Please take advice from a qualified tax professional.

Business Licensing

Business Licenses in the UAE

The licence you need depends on what your business does and where it is based. Here is an overview of the main types.

Commercial License

What is it? A licence for trading in goods.

Who needs it? Traders, importers, exporters and retailers.

Typical activities: buying, selling, distribution and trading of permitted goods.

Professional License

What is it? A licence for service activities based on professional skill.

Who needs it? Consultants and other professionals.

Typical activities: consultancy, design, advisory and other professional services.

Industrial License

What is it? A licence for production and manufacturing.

Who needs it? Factories, workshops and processing businesses.

Typical activities: manufacturing and processing. Extra approvals often apply.

General Trading License

What is it? A trading licence covering several product categories.

Who needs it? Businesses trading in a mix of goods.

Typical activities: trading in the product lists the authority allows.

E-commerce License

What is it? A licence for selling products online.

Who needs it? Online stores and marketplace sellers.

Typical activities: online retail. Some goods need additional approvals.

Service License

What is it? A licence for business service activities.

Who needs it? Agencies, IT, marketing and support businesses.

Typical activities: service activities on the authority’s approved list.

Documents Required (indicative)

  • Passport copies of owners and managers
  • Proof of address and contact details
  • Proposed trade name and activity details
  • Tenancy or office documents, where required
  • External approvals for regulated activities, where applicable

Licence FAQs

Which licence do I need for my business?

It depends on your activity. Trading, professional services and manufacturing each fall under different licence types. Tell us what your business does and we will help you identify the right one.

Can I have more than one activity on a licence?

Often yes, but the combinations allowed depend on the authority and the activities chosen.

Which UAE business setup option is suitable for me?

It depends on your activity, where your customers are, your budget and your visa needs. Mainland, free zone and offshore each suit different goals. Share your plans and we can help you compare the options.

What is the difference between mainland and free zone setup?

A mainland licence is issued by an emirate’s economic department and generally allows trading across the UAE market, subject to the licensed activity. A free zone licence is issued by the zone authority, follows that zone’s rules and activity list, and may limit direct dealings in the local market. Rules vary by authority.
