Who We Are

A UAE-based team focused on clear, dependable support

Alphytix BUSINESS SOLUTION provides business setup, visa and corporate support for individuals and companies establishing or operating in the UAE.

Setting up and running a business involves many procedures, authorities and documents. We aim to make that process easier to understand, so you can make informed decisions and focus on your business.

What you can expect from us

  • Plain-language explanations of requirements and options.
  • Organised checklists for the documents you need.
  • Regular updates on where your application stands.
  • Honest advice, including when a route may not suit your plans.
What We Do

Support across setup, residency and compliance

We assist with the administrative and procedural side of doing business in the UAE, working with the relevant authorities on your behalf where required.

Business setup

Guidance on mainland, free zone and offshore options, licensing and company formation steps.

Visa and residency

Assistance with Golden Visa enquiries, Emirates ID procedures and related residency paperwork.

Corporate services

Support with banking, accounting, VAT, corporate tax, insurance, renewals, amendments and closures.

We assist with procedures. Decisions, approvals and processing times rest with the relevant UAE authorities and cannot be guaranteed.

UAE Business Expertise

Familiar with how business works in the UAE

The UAE offers several routes for establishing and operating a business, and requirements differ between mainland, free zone and offshore structures, and between authorities.

We keep up with the procedures that apply to your case and confirm current requirements with the relevant authority before you proceed, because rules, fees and criteria can change.

  • Mainland, free zone and offshore company options.
  • Trade licensing, renewals and amendments.
  • Residency, Golden Visa and Emirates ID procedures.
  • Banking, accounting, VAT and corporate tax support.
  • Government documentation and PRO services.
