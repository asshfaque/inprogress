1. Introduction

Alphytix Business Solutions is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Alphytix Business Solutions collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request our services, or communicate with us.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Nationality or country of residence, where relevant to the requested service
  • Business or company name
  • Other information voluntarily provided through enquiry forms or communications

Business and Service Information

When you request our services, we may collect information about your proposed business, business activities, licensing requirements, ownership structure, and relevant supporting documents.

Technical Information

When you visit our website, we may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and website usage data through cookies or similar technologies, where applicable.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Provide business setup, company formation, licensing, and corporate support services.
  • Prepare quotations, proposals, and service documentation.
  • Contact you regarding your business requirements.
  • Coordinate with relevant authorities, service providers, and authorised business partners when necessary to deliver requested services.
  • Improve our website, customer experience, and service quality.
  • Manage business operations and customer relationships.
  • Send relevant marketing communications where permitted by applicable law.
  • Fulfil legal, regulatory, and compliance obligations.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorised access.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable under relevant data protection laws, we process personal information based on one or more of the following grounds:

  • Your consent.
  • Performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Compliance with legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Legitimate business interests, provided these do not override your applicable privacy rights.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant information with:

  • Government authorities, licensing departments, and regulatory bodies where necessary to provide requested services or comply with applicable law.
  • Mainland licensing authorities, free zones, and other relevant service organisations.
  • Professional advisers, consultants, and authorised business partners.
  • Technology, website hosting, communication, and customer management service providers.
  • Other parties where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.

We limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

6. Data Protection and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Access to personal information is restricted to authorised individuals and service providers who need it for legitimate business purposes.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete it or anonymise it, where appropriate and legally permissible.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support functionality, understand visitor activity, improve website performance, and measure marketing effectiveness.

Where required by applicable law, we will seek your consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings or any cookie preference tool available on our website. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

9. Marketing Communications

We may contact you about our services, offers, and relevant business updates where permitted by applicable law.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions provided or contacting us directly.

Opting out of marketing communications will not prevent us from sending essential service-related or legally required communications.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request a copy of certain personal information in a portable format, where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

11. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services. These third parties may operate under their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

Alphytix Business Solutions is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information with them.

12. International Data Transfers

Where necessary to provide our services, personal information may be processed or transferred outside the country in which it was collected.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure that such transfers comply with relevant data protection laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

13. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals and businesses seeking professional business services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so would violate applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us unlawfully, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, business practices, technology, or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
